Four ways of thinking

Higher-order thinking brings together four skills. Each has its own colour on this site, so you always know which one a course or puzzle is building.

Problem solving

How do I get from here to there?

Define the real problem, break it into parts you can handle, and test solutions before committing to them.

Problem solving courses

Critical thinking

Is this actually true?

Weigh evidence, spot weak arguments and hidden assumptions, and separate what you know from what you’ve been told.

Critical thinking courses

Innovative thinking

What else could this be?

Generate more ideas than feel comfortable, borrow from other fields, and turn the unexpected into something useful.

Innovative thinking courses

Decision making

Which path, and why?

Compare options against clear criteria, account for risk, and make choices you can explain and defend.

Climb past memorising

Most studying stops at remembering and understanding. Higher-order thinking is the top of the ladder: analysing, evaluating and creating.

Every lesson asks you to do one of those three things with what you’ve just learned – that’s where understanding sticks.

  1. Remember recall facts
  2. Understand explain ideas
  3. Apply use in new situations
  4. Analyse break apart, compare
  5. Evaluate judge and justify
  6. Create produce something new
